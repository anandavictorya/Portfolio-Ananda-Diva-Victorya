Spatial statistics · Java IslandManuscript in revision

Where and why paddy fields occur across Java

A geographically weighted logistic regression (GWLR) of paddy field presence on Java using seven physical and accessibility variables. The model estimates a separate relationship at every location, then maps which variable matters most in each area.

Share of 200 test locations where each variable is significant

  • Slope56.5%
  • Dist. to rivers34.5%
  • Dist. to roads27.0%
  • Land surface temp.25.5%
  • Rainfall16.5%
  • Aspect, soil type0%

Slope and distance from rivers are negatively related to paddy presence at every test location. The other variables change sign across the island.

Accuracy on independent test data

ModelAccuracyAUC
GWLR (local)75.5%0.818
Binary logistic (global)75.0%0.804

The local model matches the global model on accuracy (AUC difference not significant, DeLong p = 0.385) and adds a map of spatial variation. Adaptive bisquare kernel, bandwidth 175 chosen by AIC, 500 training and 200 test points, fitted in GWR4.

Web GIS · Central Kalimantan · Demo

Smart WebGIS: economic inequality in Central Kalimantan

An interactive web map of the 14 regencies and cities of Central Kalimantan. It shows GDP per capita, population density and the Williamson Index for 2022 to 2024, with a year selector, heatmap layers with adjustable radius and opacity, and a detail view for each area.

  • Williamson Index classes: low (0 to 0.3), medium (0.4 to 0.6) and high (0.7 to 1).
  • GDP per capita grouped into four bands, from under 55 million to over 100 million rupiah.
  • Figures in this demo are simulated values based on BPS data and the Williamson Index method.
